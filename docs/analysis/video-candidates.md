# Video-number candidates (X8, 2026-09-26)

Candidate lines for the 5-minute video, drawn from phase 0 (Q1–Q7) and the explorations X1–X7 and X9 (X10 was still
running and is not included). None of them is in `docs/video-numbers.md` yet; that file does not exist. Every number
below comes from the doc section named in its row. The only new arithmetic is the re-check at the end.

**Verified (X6)** means `x6_verify_claims.md` re-derived the number from the raw files, without the models or the
parsers. "Input only" means X6 confirmed the number the claim starts from, but not the model result built on it.

**Order.** Rows are ordered by strength:

- **A**: non-obvious, with every number verified by X6.
- **B**: non-obvious, but the headline number is a model result or an input X6 did not re-derive.
- **C**: verified, but it is the premise or context, not an insight.
- **D**: depends on a pending decision, or the claim is weak.

**R*n*** is item *n* of the [review queue in findings.md](findings.md#review-queue-for-pablo).

## Do not say

- "A demand record of ~87–91 GW" in January 2026. The record then was 85,508 MW, set in 2023 (R6).
- "ERCOT's forecast missed by more than 20 GW" without "preliminary" and without ERCOT's own 90.5–98 GW range. The
  margin over 20 GW is only 866 MW.
- "438 GW" without naming the queue. Generation (Aug 2026 GIS) and large load (May 2026) both come to ~438 GW.
- "3.8% of the large loads are running". It is approval to energize, not consumption.
- "The new data centers land in co-op territory". Q4 found 46.6% (R7).
- Any site or company as the source of WEST's 2026 block. TCEQ records only show permits (R9, X1 review item 6).
- Any 4CP dollar figure. No $/kW-yr rate is in the repo (R11).
- "80% band" for our forecast. The P10–P90 covered 61% of the backtest cells (R13).
- Causes for NORTH's 2009 drop or its 2022 jump. They are not verified (X1).
- The old "34 of 37" data-center count. The current selection gives 35 of 38 (Q4).
- Anything about where the held-out accounts would rank (validation lock).

## Re-check done for this list

- **X2 §3, developer dates.** In the Jan 2023 row, 141,880 ÷ 18,428 = 7.70, which is +670%, but the doc prints +570%.
  The other two rows check (+660% and +412%), and so do every raw-queue and model error in that table. Developer COD
  dates therefore came to **5.1–7.7× the MW actually built** (+412% to +670%). Fixed in X2 (the cell now reads +670%, the
  headline "5–8×"). For the video, "several times what was built" is still the safe line.
- B8: "tripled" is 201.0 ÷ 66.7 = 3.0. D5: 7.40 ÷ 3.27 = 2.3 and 9.93 ÷ 3.71 = 2.7. A1: 8,786 ÷ 26,836 = 0.33.
  A2: 112,000 − 91,134 = 20,866.
